01 — The Mark

A cross that listens, a lotus that holds.

Primary mark · Plum 01a

The Primary Lockup

The mark weaves a medical cross, a woman's profile in negative space, a stethoscope formed by her flowing hair, and a lotus carrier behind it all — a single figure for clinical care, women's health and compassionate dialogue. Always presented in Cream on Plum.

02 — Clear Space

04 — Typography

Set in Lora.

Hierarchy & voice

Lora is the single brand voice — a contemporary serif with honest proportions and a generous italic. Italics are reserved for the brand's reflective register: quotes, chapter openers, and the second half of any promise.

Display · 72-140
Listen well.
Pullquote · 28-48
“A conversation is a kind of medicine.”
Section · 32-48
On being heard.
Body · 14-18
Therapy begins the moment we agree to sit still together.

Do

Pair Lora Regular with Lora Italic. Use Italic sparingly. Set body at a measure of 55–70 characters. Keep tracking default; rely on weight and italics for hierarchy.

05 — Iconography Inside the Mark

Four symbols, one promise.

A

The Medical Cross

The central plum cross anchors the mark in clinical authority — the universal symbol for healthcare, expertise and healing. It carries the wordmark and grounds the composition.

B

The Female Profile

Formed in negative space against the cross, a woman's face appears in quiet profile. A deliberate signal that the practice is rooted in women's health and shaped by a woman's point of view.

C

The Stethoscope

Her hair flows downward and becomes the tubing of a stethoscope, ending in a small circular chest-piece. A doctor who listens — both clinically and in conversation.

D

The Lotus Carrier

A blooming lotus holds everything — petals of purity, rebirth and holistic wellness that soften the clinical cross and set the tone: expert care, offered with compassion.

07 — Misuse

The mark holds together when we let it.

✕
Don't place on busy backgroundsCompromises legibility and tone.
✕
Don't stretch or distortLock the aspect ratio to 1:1.
✕
Don't rotate the markThe figure must sit upright.
✕
Don't recolour to off-brand huesUse only approved colourways.
✕
Don't add shadows or effectsNo bevels, glows, outlines.
✕
Don't reproduce below 32 pxReach for the monogram instead.
✕
Don't use low-contrast pairingsKeep plum and cream apart.
✕
Don't lower opacity as a styleThe mark is either present or absent.
