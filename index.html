02 — About

The doctor who listens.

Javith Jalill — known to clients as Dr Jaavi — is a SLAMC registered special physician and clinical psychologist based in Colombo, working in long-form, integrative therapy shaped around the person rather than the textbook.

“ The work isn't to fix you. It's to sit with you, gently, until the next true sentence becomes possible.

Dr Jaavi is also the founder of The Chiththa Advanced Psychological Studies Open Institute of Sri Lanka — an academic project built on the same conviction that carries the practice: that therapy works best when clients are heard fully, paced carefully, and offered choices rather than instructions.

03 · The Approach
03 — The Approach

Four symbols, one promise.

The mark you see at the top of this page is more than a logo — it is the practice in miniature.

A Featured

The Cross

Clinical rigour. Real, properly trained therapy — evidence-based and never improvised. The cross holds the centre of the mark for a reason.

B

The Profile

You at the centre. Sessions designed around the person in front of us, not a protocol on a page.

C

The Stethoscope

Listening, twice. Once for what is being said, and again for what is sitting just beneath it.

D

The Lotus

Compassion as method. Holistic, unhurried, and held with the warmth a therapy room should always have.
