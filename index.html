02 — About

The doctor who listens.

Dr. Jaavi is a counseling psychologist based in Colombo, working in long-form, integrative therapy — bringing together CBT, psychodynamic and humanistic approaches in a way that fits the person, not the textbook.

The practice was founded in 2019 on a simple idea — that therapy works best when clients are heard fully, paced carefully, and offered choices rather than instructions.

03 · The Approach
03 — The Approach

Four symbols, one promise.

The mark you see at the top of this page is more than a logo — it is the practice in miniature.

A

The Cross

Clinical rigour. Real, properly trained therapy — evidence-based and never improvised.

B

The Profile

You at the centre. Sessions designed around the person in front of us, not a protocol on a page.

C

The Stethoscope

Listening, twice. Once for what is being said, and again for what is sitting just beneath it.

D

The Lotus

Compassion as method. Holistic, unhurried, and held with the warmth a therapy room should always have.
